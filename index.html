About Us

We saw reading support not reaching the students who need it most, so we built a tool that meets them at their level, even offline.

Our background

87% of learners struggle to read at grade level, and only 12.58% of senior high learners read independently. Teachers with 40+ students per class have little time and few tools to help each reader. We created BasaWisyo to close that gap, even where internet is weak.

Our mission

To help Filipino students in Grades 7â€“12 grow into confident readers through leveled, offline, collaborative practice, while giving teachers fast, measurable results with less paperwork.

Our vision

A country where every Filipino learner reads at their grade level and beyond, in the classroom or offline, with no student left behind.

Products & Services

BasaWisyo: six tools, one reading journey, from the first placement test to the DepEd report.

Phil-IRI Placement

Identify each student's reading level and set a measurable baseline.

Personalized Practice

Leveled English and Filipino passages, questions, instant feedback, and Tap-a-Word.

Bayanihan Class Quests

A shared classroom mission: students contribute together, not compete.

BasaMesh

Share passages, stories, and quizzes by Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, with no internet.

DepEd Reports & Form 14

Turns Phil-IRI progress data into ready-to-print DepEd reports and Form 14.

Teacher Dashboard

See reading levels, weak skills, quest participation, and report readiness in one place.
